# STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE: OPENING AND RUNNING IN ANDROID STUDIO

### PROJECT: TASMAC SHOP NO. 11066 – ANNADURAI M

The `android` directory is a **complete, native Android Studio project** ready to open, build, and run directly on your Android phone.

---

## 🚀 3 EASY STEPS TO INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE VIA ANDROID STUDIO

### STEP 1: OPEN IN ANDROID STUDIO
1. Open **Android Studio**.
2. Click **File** > **Open...** (or click **Open** on the welcome screen).
3. Browse to this project folder:
   ```
   C:\Users\Lenovo\Desktop\TASMAC\android
   ```
4. Click **OK**. Android Studio will load the project and sync Gradle files automatically.

---

### STEP 2: CONNECT YOUR ANDROID PHONE
1. On your Android phone, enable **Developer Options**:
   - Go to **Settings** > **About phone** > tap **Build number** 7 times until you see *"You are now a developer!"*.
2. Enable **USB Debugging**:
   - Go to **Settings** > **Developer options** > toggle on **USB debugging**.
3. Plug your phone into your laptop via USB cable.
4. On your phone screen, tap **"Allow USB debugging"**.
5. Your phone will appear in the top device selector bar in Android Studio!

---

### STEP 3: RUN AND INSTALL THE APP
1. In Android Studio, click the green **Run (▶)** button in the top toolbar (or press `Shift + F10`).
2. Android Studio will build the app and install it directly onto your phone!
3. The app will launch immediately with the **TASMAC SHOP NO. 11066 – WELCOME ANNADURAI M** opening splash screen!

---

## 📦 ALTERNATIVE: GENERATE STANDALONE APK (WITHOUT USB)

If you want to generate an installable `.apk` file that you can send to your phone via WhatsApp or USB drive:

1. In Android Studio top menu, click:
   **Build** > **Build Bundle(s) / APK(s)** > **Build APK(s)**.
2. Wait a few moments until you see a notification in the bottom right corner:
   `APK(s) generated successfully for 1 module`.
3. Click **"locate"** in that notification.
4. Your APK file will be located at:
   ```
   android\app\build\outputs\apk\debug\app-debug.apk
   ```
5. Send this file to your phone, tap it, and click **Install**!

---

## 🔒 100% OFFLINE CONFIRMATION
- Once installed on your phone, **disconnect the USB cable and turn off all Wi-Fi and Mobile Data**.
- Open the app. Everything works 100% offline:
  - Daily Remittance calculations and saving.
  - One common bottle inventory tracking.
  - Vendor handover receipt generation and thermal printing.
  - All records are permanently stored in your phone's internal storage and will remain intact after phone restarts!
